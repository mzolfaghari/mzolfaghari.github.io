Reza Zolfaghari

Learning to connect what we see, hear and say: multimodal models, from research to products.

I lead multimodal AI at Huawei, where my team works on understanding and generating video, images and music. Before that I co-led the on-device vision-language work at Tether AI, and spent four years as Head of AI/ML and CTO at Zebracat, where we trained video diffusion models and ran them in production. Earlier I was an Applied Scientist at Amazon. I did my PhD on multimodal video understanding with Thomas Brox at the University of Freiburg, and my papers have appeared at NeurIPS, ICCV and ECCV.
